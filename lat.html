LAT

Art and Technology Laboratories

Since 2017, with support from the Ludwig Foundation of Cuba, Milton Raggi Vinueza, in collaboration with AXIS.Visual Lab, has developed the Art and Technology Laboratories (LAT) project.

LAT aims to promote workshops, collaborative initiatives, and exchange platforms that foster the professional development of emerging creators and engage new audiences across Cuba.
